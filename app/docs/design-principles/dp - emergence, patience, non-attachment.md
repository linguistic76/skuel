There is a call for a clear file of **development principles**—distinct from design or philosophical principles. The first two proposed principles are:

### 4. Build prepared environments for emergence.

Growth cannot be forced in an authoritarian way.  
It can be invited, supported, and made more likely through the quality of the environment. SKUEL should focus on shaping conditions in which insight, discipline, calm, and growth can emerge naturally. The environment is not passive; it is participatory and alive.

### 5. Start from lived reality, not abstraction.

Begin where the person actually is: body, nervous system, habits, confusion, stress, longing, relationships, daily life.  
SKUEL should not float above life in conceptual language. It should ground philosophy in experience, practice, and observable patterns. The door opens through what is lived, not through what merely sounds elevated.
